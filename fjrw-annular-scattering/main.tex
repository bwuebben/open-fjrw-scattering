\begin{abstract}
For each finite labelled collection of insertions and each deformation order,
we construct a compact oriented rational piecewise-linear bordism class over
the moduli spaces of open and closed quartic $W$-spin curves.  Its cells
parametrize decorated tropical forests in a cut-open integral-affine annulus
together with the corresponding $W$-spin data.  Applying a transported
relative residue trace to its zero-dimensional endpoint chains defines the
contact moments.  After the canonical identifications of internal faces, the
exterior boundary consists of the two annular ends, closed-component
splittings, wall-crossing and cotangent-line faces, pairwise exchange faces,
and closed factors of incompatible Euler degree.  The residue trace vanishes
on the wall and degree-mismatched contributions, while exchange contributions
cancel by orientation.  Stokes' formula gives the two Gross--Kelly--Tessler
cotangent-line recursions.  Their difference strictly lowers the number of
labels, so the vacuum and one-marking values determine all moments and
identify them with the Gross--Kelly--Tessler invariants.  The two annular ends
give the least-slope and greatest-slope canonical boundary conditions.  As
the finite data vary, the bordism classes form an inverse system equivariant
for the annular core holonomy.  They therefore form a holonomy-equivariant
inverse system of theta-decorated relative PL bordism classes whose
coefficientwise contact moments give the complete formal genus-zero
descendent potential.

The affine annulus and its coefficient system are determined by the same
quartic data.  Applying the cofactor map to the first one-marking descendent
generators produces an index-eight extension of the toric character lattice, and
the minimal cover preserving this lattice is an annulus with four
focus--focus singularities and hyperbolic core holonomy of trace $18$.
Transport of the required Kummer roots produces an inverse system of finite
root extensions.  The twisted volume line carries a Jacobi--Kirillov bracket;
Saito's primitive form enters separately in the primary comparison.  The two
descendent Hamiltonians generate a consistent scattering diagram at every
finite deformation order.

Resolving the annular boundary and attaching six integral-affine cylinders
gives separated fine-saturated logarithmic compactifications with flat
boundary divisors.  A dominating system obtained by logarithmic resolution,
principalization, and root constructions is log smooth with regular geometric
fibers.  The annular
charts also transport the narrow-primary
open-FJRW Frobenius structure.  The construction is coefficientwise: it does
not give an analytic virtual fundamental class for the infinite system or
identify a coefficient in an arbitrary GKT chamber.
\end{abstract}
