\begin{abstract}
For the open FJRW theory of Gross, Kelly and Tessler attached to the quartic
Fermat polynomial in two variables, we prove that, with its two monomials
matched to toric divisors of $\PP(1,1,2)$, the smallest cover of the tropical
base of Carl, Pumperla and Siebert on which the affine monodromy preserves the
lattice spanned by the exponents of the base and of the first-descendent wall-crossing generators of
twists $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ is the double cover branched at two of its three
singular points, an integral-affine annulus.  Its core holonomy is
hyperbolic, and lifting its transitions requires roots of the slab functions
that generate an infinite Kummer extension under transport.  The annulus
carries a wall structure, consistent at its two joints in a sense weaker than
that of Gross and Siebert, whose wall-crossing agrees with that of Gross,
Kelly and Tessler between the extremal systems of invariants for a single
marking but not for two; the difference does not change periods, and its
removal from the annular structure is left open.  The homotopies of boundary
conditions realizing this wall-crossing ray by ray can be chosen compatibly
for all markings.
\end{abstract}
