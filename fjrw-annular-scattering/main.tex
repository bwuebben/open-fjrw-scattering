\begin{abstract}
For the open FJRW theory of $x^4+y^4$ of Gross, Kelly and Tessler (GKT), we
prove that the first-descendent wall-crossing generators force the
Carl--Pumperla--Siebert base of $\PP(1,1,2)$ to be replaced by an
integral-affine annulus, a double cover branched at two of its three singular
points.  Precisely, with the two monomials of the potential matched to the
coordinate divisors of weight one, the annulus is the smallest cover on
which the affine monodromy preserves the lattice spanned by the exponents of
the base and those of the two generators of a single internal marking of
twist $(1,0)$ or $(0,1)$; only one of the three local monodromies preserves
it.  On the annulus the core holonomy is hyperbolic, and the slab functions
need roots whose transports generate an infinite Kummer extension.  The
annulus carries a wall structure consistent at its two joints, in a sense
weaker than that of Gross and Siebert.  On the two generators its
wall-crossing agrees with the GKT transition between the extremal boundary
conditions, but not on the first wall carrying one marking of each; the
difference leaves periods unchanged and is removed by a normal form of
unknown geometric origin.  The GKT homotopies realizing this transition can
be chosen compatibly as markings are added.
\end{abstract}
