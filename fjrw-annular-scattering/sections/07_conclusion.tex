\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

The principal result is the relative PL broken-line chain over the finite
diagrams of quartic open and closed $W$-spin moduli spaces.  Its exterior
boundary realizes the two quartic cotangent recursions by Stokes' formula, and
their difference reduces every multi-label contact moment to smaller
supports.  Vacuum and singleton values, together with the compact closed
factors, therefore determine the moment family.  Comparison with
Gross--Kelly--Tessler identifies these moments and, by triangularity, the two
canonical extreme endpoint systems.  Literal restriction across finite
windows organizes the formal descendent class as a $K$-equivariant inverse
system in theta-decorated relative PL bordism; its coefficientwise contact
moments give the descendent potential.

The annular affine, pro-Kummer, and Jacobi constructions provide the geometry
needed for that chain.  Descendent exponents force the annular parity cover,
transported slab roots force the pro-Kummer system, and the twisted volume
form gives a volume line carrying the Jacobi bracket.  In these categories the
initial Hamiltonians have a contractibly consistent completion, broken-line
products form a framed theta-algebra local system, and logarithmic caps add
separated fs finite-stage boundaries while retaining wall transport at the
two ends.

The supporting comparison with open FJRW theory is a factorization statement,
not an equality of wall functions.  The GKT critical factors embed in the
annular Jacobi group, while the residual factors are those required by
scattering consistency; the compatible-realization theorem supplies finite
GKT homotopies for the enumerative factors.  Gross--Kelly--Tessler's primary
mirror theorem supplies the source Saito/FJRW tensor.  The contribution here
is its transport, including the primitive form, Kodaira--Spencer basis,
multiplication, unit, and residue pairing, through every framed annular and
finite Kummer chart.

Two limitations remain.  The pro-Kummer compactification is not identified
with a distinguished toric degeneration of the original quartic pair.  The
finite-window PL construction also does not produce an analytic
infinite-window virtual fundamental class.  Moreover, chamber-independent
moments cannot determine a raw coefficient in an arbitrary chamber; the
coefficient-level statement is deliberately restricted to the two specified
extreme normal forms.
