\section{Notation}
\label{app:notation}

The principal symbols, with the place where each is defined.  Local symbols
used in a single proof are not listed.  The letter $T$ with a singular point
as index, as in $T_A,T_B,T_C$, denotes a monodromy of the disk; otherwise
$T$, also as an index, denotes a finite rooted subtree of the path tree
$\Tcal$.  The sans-serif letters $\mathsf A,\mathsf B$ denote the singleton
Hamiltonian vector fields, and the roman letters $A,B,C$ singular points;
exceptions are GKT's rings $A_I$, $A_{\mathcal I}$, $A^{\mathrm{prim}}_I$ and
functional $A(J,\mathbf d,\nu)$, the ring $A$ of the root-stack convention
of Appendix~\ref{app:calculations}, the label subsets $A$ and graph bases
$B\Lambda$ of Appendix~\ref{app:gkt-completion}, the base $B$ of
\cite{grosssiebert2011,carletal2024}, the logarithms $C_{\mathfrak r}$ of ray
factors (Section~\ref{sec:jacobi-theta}) and the corrections $C_i$ of
Appendix~\ref{app:residual-coherence}.

\emph{Lattice conventions.}  The fan lattice is called $M$ here because it
carries the exponents.  The following dictionary compares the conventions
with those of toric geometry and of
\cite{grosssiebert2011,carletal2024}; in the latter, walls lie on a base $B$
and are tangent to their exponents.
